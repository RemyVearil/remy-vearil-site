M.Eng. thesis · Cornell · Advisor: Prof. Lindsay Anderson

Heating greenhouses with data center waste heat

Almost all the power a data center uses ends up as heat, and most of it is thrown away. My thesis asks whether that heat can keep a nearby greenhouse warm year-round.

I'm modeling heat generation, recovery, storage, and delivery in MATLAB across four cooling scenarios, and using machine learning to generate predictive seasonal schedules that match greenhouse heating demand to data center operations.
